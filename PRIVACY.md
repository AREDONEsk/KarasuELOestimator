# Karasu ELO Estimator – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Karasu ELO Estimator is an unofficial browser extension for karasu.live. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or made by Karasu.

## Summary

The extension does not collect, sell, or share any personal data. It does not send any data to the developer or to anyone else. Everything it does happens inside your own browser.

## What the extension reads

On karasu.live match pages, the extension reads the team names and the Elo value shown for each player. It uses them only to calculate each team's win chance and the estimated Elo gain or loss, and shows the result on the same page. This information never leaves your browser.

## What the extension stores

The extension saves only your own settings, using Chrome's built-in extension storage:

- whether the overlay is turned on
- your usual HLTV rating, used for the estimate
- whether auto-accept is turned on, and the delay before accepting

These settings stay in your Chrome profile. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome may sync them between your own devices, as it does for any extension setting. The developer cannot see them.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party.
- It makes no network requests of its own.
- It does not read your karasu.live login, passwords, or account details.
- It does not track your browsing; it only runs on karasu.live.
- It does not use analytics, advertising, or cookies.

## Auto-accept

If you turn on auto-accept, the extension clicks karasu.live's own accept button when a match is found, after the delay you set. It does not record this or send it anywhere. It is off until you turn it on.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its saved settings.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new date.
